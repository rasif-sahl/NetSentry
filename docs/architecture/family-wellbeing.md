# Family profiles, policies and observed wellbeing

Phase 11, 16 November 2026. Source implementation and synthetic integration evidence do not certify child identity, platform enforcement or phone behavior.

## Family scope and consent

Create a Family workspace and use the existing invitation workflow for guardians (owner-granted `GUARDIAN`) and linked child accounts (`MEMBER`). Owners/admins manage profiles and grants; a guardian sees/manages only profiles that explicitly name their current verified account. A linked member can read their own child profile, give feedback, request time and withdraw sharing. Viewers have no child-profile access. Current membership/account/role is read on every operation under the existing workspace lock. Guardian status never grants developer process control.

Profiles use a display name, bounded age band and built-in avatar icon, avoiding image upload and birth-date storage. Labels and account linkage do not verify age or legal relationships. Network associations are workspace labels; they do not identify a child's traffic or grant router authority. Profile scope is independent of ordinary shared network metadata.

Only the currently active device owner can accept `family-sharing-v1` for a device they enrolled. Sharing does not enable collection, grant usage access, expose pairing credentials or establish enforcement permission. Each assignment binds device generation **and profile revision**. Profile edits require the current `expectedRevision`, preventing stale forms from restoring revoked grants. Profile changes invalidate existing sharing; the device owner must renew consent. Changing the linked child account removes old assignments, requests and family timeline entries. Re-enrolment, collection-consent changes, pause and revocation change device generation and invalidate assignments. Child/guardian/owner withdrawal removes the association and receipt while personal measurements remain separate. A device owner can also use the independent account-authenticated `/devices/:id/family-assignment` deletion route after losing a profile grant or workspace membership. This grants no profile access and is idempotent. Invalid old bindings do not consume the twenty-current-device limit.

Generic shared usage/application report routes cannot bypass family scope: a guardian's legacy reports remain limited to their independently owned devices. Shared legacy alert rules for guardians in a Family workspace are rejected/rechecked at display and delivery. Child reports use the new scoped family path and consent-date filtering. Owners/admins retain their existing workspace report authority. No role or capability alone authorizes a restriction.

## Policy intent, schedules and exceptions

One current policy per child contains a revision, author, timezone and bounded rules: daily observed screen-time allowance, app/category limits, allowed/restricted application IDs, bedtime/study/weekend schedules, a temporary pause (up to 24 hours), network pause, domain/category filter selections and enabled state. Age bands suggest editable starting points; they never activate a policy automatically. Daily limits are 1–1,440 minutes; up to twelve schedules and bounded app/domain lists are accepted. Unknown nested keys, arbitrary actions, malformed domains, contradictory allow/restrict lists and invalid schedules are rejected.

Saving requires explicit disclosure acceptance and `expectedRevision`; stale edits return conflict. Timezone is captured from the workspace when saving. Report/usage evaluation for a policy uses that same captured zone; save a new revision after changing workspace timezone. Schedule start is inclusive/end exclusive. Overnight schedules use the preceding start day after midnight, including weekend wrap. Actual local wall time handles DST repeated/skipped hours. Missing usage remains unknown, not zero. Threshold warnings begin at 80%; observed thresholds produce requested intent, never an applied action.

Linked child accounts create immutable UUID requests for 1–120 minutes, expiring after 24 hours. Assigned guardians or family owners/admins approve/reject; the requester can cancel pending requests. Only the first decision wins under the workspace lock. Reusing a request UUID with altered content conflicts. Acceptance/approval is a stored decision, not device execution. Approval grants at most the requested duration from decision time. Current author/approver grants and exact policy revision are rechecked; changed policy, role withdrawal, archive or expiry removes effective exceptions. Guardians can also explicitly record a bounded direct exception. Decisions and policy/consent changes have timeline events. Historical approved records may remain visible after an exception ceases to be effective.

## Synchronization and enforcement boundary

The paired device uses its opaque installation credential for `/devices/:id/family/configuration` and `/receipt`; account JWTs cannot substitute. The service rechecks current workspace/device state and generation inside its transaction. A missing/currently invalid assignment returns null. Receipts bind device generation, profile revision and policy revision; only `unsupported`, `failed` and `not_requested` are accepted. `applied` is deliberately rejected. A receipt is a device report; after ninety seconds it is unconfirmed in the family view. Repeated identical status updates do not create repeated timeline events. Failed reports create an enforcement-failure event.

| Adapter boundary | Current result | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `android-consumer-v1` | Unsupported restrictions | The current consumer app observes UsageStats; it is not provisioned as a device/profile owner or authorized package-management delegate. No app suspension, accessibility workaround, VPN/DNS filtering or device-admin provisioning is added. |
| `openwrt-ubus-v1` | Unsupported restrictions | The Phase 10 adapter is read-only and hardware-unverified. No firewall, network pause or DNS mutation is authorized or executed. |
| iOS | Phase 12 | Apple authorization/entitlement-dependent source follows separately. |

These adapters expose an empty supported-action list and fail closed. Restriction mechanisms require a separately supported/provisioned authority and physical validation before an executing adapter is enabled. A saved rule, guardian decision, monitoring permission or capability report cannot supply that authority. Current family flows implement warnings, intent, revision synchronization, unsupported/failure reporting and audit, **not functioning device restrictions**. Native notifications and actual enforcement remain unavailable/pending.

Android `FamilyWire` validates the received assignment/generation and can emit only unsupported/not-requested receipts. Repository policy checks run separately from collection and do not restart monitoring. Identity revocation still propagates and clears pairing/queues; ordinary policy transport failure does not fabricate execution. Family views clear on account/workspace/authorization changes. Family polling is visible/resumed only; background cadence depends on already enabled collection/work and remains a physical-device check.

References: [Android UsageStatsManager](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/usage/UsageStatsManager), [DevicePolicyManager package suspension and authority](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/admin/DevicePolicyManager#setPackagesSuspended(android.content.ComponentName,%20java.lang.String[],%20boolean)).

## Reports and evidence

Family reports read accepted estimated Android aggregates only for currently valid assignments. They include dates strictly after the consented local date, so the first full local day is the earliest shareable day. Historical/pre-assignment data is not silently backfilled. The fourteen-day view provides two seven-day totals, daily coverage and late-night observations, up to thirty-two app/category trends, education/entertainment comparisons, descriptive suggestions and feedback. Unclassified applications stay unknown. Custom catalogue category UUIDs work in policy limits. Education requires an explicit `education` purpose on a category-limit entry; `entertainment`/`other` purposes can override the default descriptive media/game grouping. The app does not infer educational value from package or category names.

A percentage comparison requires all current assignments to predate the report range, fourteen observed coverage days, positive prior usage and coverage within 10% across periods. Even then it compares observations, not complete attention or health. Devices/app windows can overlap; sums are not unique-person screen time. No wellbeing/protection score, diagnosis, cold-start count, guessed gap or guaranteed internet/content filtering is produced. Late-night observations can suggest reviewing bedtime; a stale assigned device suggests checking missing reports. These are deterministic descriptions, not automatic recommendations that change a policy.

## Retention and privacy

Migration thirteen adds six tables. Schedule `npm --prefix backend run family:work -- <workspace-uuid>` using the deployment scheduler. It removes at most 1,000 requests older than thirty days and 1,000 timeline events older than ninety days per run; repeat batches after downtime. No scheduler is installed. Profiles/assignments/current policies persist until explicit withdrawal/erasure, with limits of 32 profiles/family, 20 devices/profile and 1,000 requests/profile. Latest-only receipts are generation/revision scoped. Views return the latest hundred requests/events.

Personal export includes only the user's linked profile fields, owned-device assignments, their requests and their actor events; peer guardian arrays and other children's profiles are omitted. Account deletion erases linked child profiles, requester/actor content and guardian references; it invalidates policy authors through existing foreign keys. This stops family sharing while preserving another person's enrolled device and personal measurements. Device data erasure/removal clears its family assignment. Profile erasure cascades family records without deleting personal device measurements. Family reference locks participate in the existing ordered account-deletion transaction.

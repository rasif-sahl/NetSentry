# Business organizations and ownership

A BUSINESS workspace is one organization; an account may belong to several. Current, verified membership is checked for every protected operation. Organization/location filters are reporting dimensions, not authority to access another organization. Business-specific roles cannot be granted in a Family/Personal workspace. Legacy Business MEMBER memberships migrate to EMPLOYEE; unused legacy MEMBER invitations are revoked. Downgrade maps the new roles to read-only VIEWER and revokes their outstanding invitations.

| Role | Inventory | Organization aggregates/export | Members/devices/policies | Networks/alerts | Audit | Billing | Developer stop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Owner | Organization | Yes | Manage | Manage | Read/export | Owner gate | Separate named/local grants |
| Administrator | Organization | Yes | Manage, owner/admin grants protected | Manage | Read/export | No | Separate named/local grants |
| Network administrator | Organization metadata | No | No | Manage | Read | No | No |
| Analyst | Organization metadata | Yes | No | Read | Read/export | No | No |
| Viewer | Organization metadata | No | No | Read | No | No | No |
| Employee | Own/assigned metadata | No | Own enrolment, own sharing/privacy | No | No | No | No |

Network administrators may enrol their own authorized network collectors; this does not grant organization device administration or developer-module access.

Service viewing retains the existing explicitly shared-summary boundary for permitted viewers; no role substitutes for computer-owner/local consent. Billing permission is an authorization gate only; payments/subscriptions remain Phase 14. Roles are organization-wide. There is no implicit per-location privilege or platform support interface with global access.

Locations contain multiple networks. Observed network endpoints can also receive revision-checked department metadata without being assigned to a person. This never verifies ownership, network presence or per-device traffic; location filtering follows the network association, and cross-organization endpoint/department IDs are rejected. Departments belong to a location, employees to a department, and devices may be assigned to a current verified employee. Assignment/reassignment is not consent. Only that employee accepts `business-sharing-v1`, against the current assignment revision and displayed device generation. A department/location change invalidates affected sharing. Data enters organization reports starting with the first full workspace-local day after acceptance. Revoked membership, withdrawn/revised assignment, stale generation, offboarding or disabled monitoring excludes data. Missing rows mean unavailable, not zero. Values stay separated by metric, quality and source/version; overlapping sources must not be treated as additive. No application IDs, browsing contents or employee productivity scores enter these reports.

Generic monitoring/application reports and exports in Business workspaces are restricted to independently registered devices. Generic shared usage alerts are denied, including for an administrator, so they cannot bypass employee sharing dates through an older endpoint. Organization aggregate reports are separate consent-scoped routes. Business alert assignment works on authorized network events and only assigns current alert handlers. It does not enable a notification provider.

An employee's personal device remains registered to that employee. Managers may remotely unenrol it within the organization, revoking credentials/generation/consent, but cannot erase its personal observations. The owner can still use personal export/erasure, including after leaving. Offboarding removes organization membership, invalidates pending enrolments, revokes in-organization device/agent credentials, ends assigned-device sharing and pending commands/controller grants, and clears alert assignment. It never removes membership, devices or consent in another organization/personal workspace. Already executing actions cannot be recalled. Server access ends immediately; an offline client learns of credential revocation on its next connection, and physical cessation/purge delivery is a device acceptance gate.

Company custody is a separate `business-custody-v1` declaration, permitted only to the organization owner who actually registered that device. Neither a role nor an employee assignment can claim someone else's personal device. On organization ownership transfer, only explicitly declared company assets move registration custody to the new owner; credentials/consent are revoked and must be renewed. Ordinary devices retain their registrant. An employee's account deletion revokes assigned corporate devices and removes their employee relationships without deleting another custodian's asset. Account deletion remains blocked while an owner must transfer a shared organization. These are application custody rules; physical ownership and lawful employee consent are not verified by role names.

Compliance is a configurable review of metadata staleness, registration, reported permission and monitoring consent. `configured_checks_met` means only those checks matched; unknown/missing observations remain reviewable. It is not a certification of patching, encryption, malware protection, legal compliance or network reachability.

Business audit stores actor, action, target UUID and time, excluding credentials/freeform HR or activity data. Audit views/exports also project minimal retained network events and developer-command outcomes through organization-bound joins. Business records retain 90 days; network and developer records keep their existing 30/7-day limits. Run bounded `business:work <workspaceUUID>` maintenance alongside existing workers; no production scheduler is provisioned. Personal export projects only one's own employee/sharing/actor records, and erasure removes actor-linked Business data. Reports/exports and inventories are bounded and reject overly broad report ranges rather than silently truncating measurement results.

Native clients use the same account/enrolment APIs and sharing contract. Organization administration lives in the web dashboard. SDK-free client checks do not certify native builds, OS permissions, physical monitoring, remote unenrolment delivery or BYOD acceptance. Existing platform limitations remain unchanged; no agent/router/Apple restriction authority is added by this domain.
